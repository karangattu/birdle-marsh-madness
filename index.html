A 60-second spotting-scope challenge in the marsh.

How to play

  1. 1
    Drag the spotting scope across the marsh. Birds are tiny dots from this far away.
  2. 2
    When a bird sits inside the eyepiece, you'll see it magnified and its name button will light up.
  3. 3
    Tap the lit-up button to log the sighting. Wrong taps cost you.
  4. 4
    Find all 11 birds in 60 seconds. Faster = bigger time bonus.
